About EcoGlow

EcoGlow was founded to prove that effective skincare can also be sustainable. We combine research-backed botanicals with responsible sourcing to create products that perform without compromise.

Our Mission

To make sustainable beauty accessible by designing products with a low environmental footprint and transparent ingredient sourcing.

Sustainability Practices

  • Recycled glass packaging and refill program
  • Supplier audits for fair labor
  • Carbon-offset shipping on domestic orders

SCI Principle: Growth through Integration

We applied the Science of Creative Intelligence principle of "Growth through Integration" by harmonizing product design, customer experience, data-informed decisions, and ethical AI use. Integration guided the information architecture, visuals, and responsive layout to create a coherent user journey.

AI Workflow Narrative

For this project I used AI tools intentionally to accelerate ideation and production while preserving human oversight. I selected ChatGPT for copywriting and SEO brainstorming because it quickly generated tone-appropriate headlines and keyword variations. Canva AI produced polished hero visuals and social graphics that matched brand colors; Runway was used to generate a short, atmospheric homepage video to illustrate product use. Two exact prompts used were: (1) "Write five creative homepage taglines for an eco-friendly skincare brand targeting young professionals," and (2) "Create a 30‑second video concept script showing a morning skincare routine focused on sustainability and natural ingredients." Outputs were carefully edited to ensure factual accuracy, brand voice, and originality; images and video were fine-tuned in Canva and Runway and then exported as web-friendly assets. AI supported creativity by proposing multiple directions quickly and improving production quality for visuals. Responsible AI practices included: documenting tools and prompts, human review for bias and factual accuracy, and attribution (e.g., "Image created with Canva AI"). The integration strengthened both creative speed and strategic clarity, allowing more time to focus on user experience and SEO optimization.
